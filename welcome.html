Get Mammoth

Mammoth is on. You're protected.

Ads, trackers and scam networks are now blocked in this browser. Here's how to get the most out of it.

1. Pin Mammoth

Click the puzzle icon in your toolbar, then the pin next to Mammoth. Now it's always one click away.

2. See what was blocked

Click the Mammoth icon on any site to see how many requests were stopped and who tried.

3. Check a page for scams

Not sure about a site? Open the popup and click Check this page for scam signs.

4. Fix a broken site

If a site acts strange, switch Mammoth off for that site in the popup. It reloads and works as usual.

5. Hide cookie banners

Want fewer pop-ups? Turn on Annoyances in Mammoth's Options page.

6. Keep your settings

Moving computers? Use Export settings in Options, then import them on the new one.
